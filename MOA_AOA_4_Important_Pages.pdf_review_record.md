**Document Type:** Memorandum and Articles of Association (MOA/AOA)  

**Document Purpose:** Constitutional charter of the company setting out its objects, powers and internal governance, including the authority to acquire and exploit intellectual property, to extend financial guarantees, and to indemnify its officers and employees.  

**Key Clauses:**  
- **Intellectual Property (Page 1):** Authorises the company to develop, acquire, protect, licence, assign, transfer, sub‑licence and otherwise exploit all forms of IP (patents, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets, software, designs, domain names, etc.) worldwide for the benefit of the company.

- **Indemnity Clause – High:** Broad indemnification of senior personnel could obligate the company to bear any liability incurred by them, irrespective of fault or amount.
